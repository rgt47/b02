Data Description

Data Source

Data for this analysis come from the Alzheimer’s Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI), accessed via the ADNIMERGE2 R package (Donohue et al., 2026). ADNI is a longitudinal multicenter study designed to develop clinical, imaging, genetic, and biochemical biomarkers for early detection and tracking of Alzheimer’s disease.

Data access: adni.loni.usc.edu

Data download date: [Your download date]

Citation: Donohue MC, et al. (2026). Alzheimer’s clinical research data via R packages: The alzverse. Alzheimer’s Dement, 22:e71152.

Study Population

Inclusion Criteria:

  1. Baseline diagnosis of Mild Cognitive Impairment (EMCI or LMCI)
  2. At least 3 years of follow-up OR documented conversion to AD
  3. Complete data on primary predictor variables

Exclusion Criteria:

  1. Baseline diagnosis of CN or Dementia
  2. No follow-up visits after baseline
  3. Missing data on >50% of predictor variables

Excluded Variables

The following variable categories are intentionally excluded to maximize clinical translatability (Grassi et al., 2019):

  • Imaging: MRI volumes, PET SUVRs
  • Genetic: APOE genotype
  • Biomarkers: CSF Abeta, tau, p-tau

Rationale: These measures require specialized equipment and expertise not available in most community clinical settings.

Limitations

Scope and Generalizability

  • Results specific to ADNI population (highly educated, mostly White)
  • May not generalize to community clinic populations
  • ADNI uses strict diagnostic criteria

Methodological Limitations

  • Retrospective cohort design
  • Cannot establish causation
  • 3-year prediction horizon may miss later converters
  • No external validation cohort

Data Limitations

  • Missing data on some predictor variables
  • Potential selection bias in ADNI enrollment
  • Measurement variability across sites
